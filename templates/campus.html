How it works

  1. Choose your classTick the divisions you want to schedule.
  2. Build your weekCheck the room estimate, then select Build timetable.
  3. View or downloadOpen Timetables to see each class, teacher and room, or download a spreadsheet file.
What do the timetable terms mean?
Division
A group of students who attend classes together, such as Second Year A.
Weekly periods
How many class slots a subject needs each week.
Double lab
A practical class that takes two periods in a row.
Clash
Two classes need the same teacher, room or division at the same time.
Difficulty
How often similar plans took extra work to arrange.
